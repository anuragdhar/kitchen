# "Stand here": a camera inside the house (2026-10-06)

The owner asked for a way to put the camera at a point inside the house and look from there.

## How to use it

On a room page (Drawing Room, Lobby / Dining, Bedroom 1, Bedroom 3) or in Whole home 3D, press **Stand here**. The view
drops to eye height (1.6 m) inside the room. Then:

- **Drag** to look around.
- **Click a spot on the floor** to walk there, or use the **arrow keys or W A S D**.
- **Mouse wheel** makes the view wider or narrower.
- A strip above the picture shows where you stand: X and Z in millimetres, eye height and the direction you face. You can
  type any of them. Set eye height to about 1200 to see the room as someone seated.
- **Leave** (or any view button) puts the camera back exactly where it was.

On a room page X is measured from the west wall and Z from the north wall, and facing 0 is north. In Whole home 3D the
numbers are positions in the whole-home model, and you start in the middle of the Lobby.

## Limits

- You can walk through furniture and walls: nothing stops you except the outer edge of the room (or of the home).
- The rooms are drawn without a ceiling, so looking up shows sky, with the ceiling fittings floating.
- While standing, clicking for dimensions, marking a wall and the Measure tool are paused; they work again after Leave.
- The Study, Home Office, Main entry, Storage and Kitchen pages do not have the button yet.
