# Drawing Room sofas and rug that look like real furniture (2026-10-06)

You asked for better-quality sofas that "render more naturally". The two Drawing Room sofas, their scatter cushions and
the rug were built from plain boxes. They are now modelled like upholstered furniture, in every seating layout (A, B, B2,
B3 and C) on the Drawing Room page and in Whole home 3D. Nothing was moved, resized or re-coloured.

## What looks different

- **Soft, rounded sofas.** Rounded arms and back, three seat cushions with slightly domed tops and a fine piped edge, three
  back cushions that lean back against the frame, and a padded base.
- **Legs and a shadow gap.** Four slim tapered dark-wood legs, about 100 mm tall, with a visible gap and a soft shadow
  under the sofa. The old model's base floated about 100 mm above its legs; that is fixed.
- **Fabric that reads as fabric.** A fine woven texture (generated by the app itself, no image files) gives the cream
  upholstery a linen-like surface up close, with the soft sheen fabric has at a glancing angle.
- **Scatter cushions that sit and lean.** The six cushions (two terracotta, two ochre, two sand) are now filled cushions
  with sewn edges. They stand on the seat and lean against the back cushions: a large terracotta one in each corner, an
  ochre one beside it, and a smaller sand one in front. Before, they were six thin slabs in a row, floating in front of the
  back.
- **A wool rug.** Same size and the same two colours (terracotta border, warm tan field), now with a rounded bound edge, a
  short-pile surface, a thin light stripe inside the border and a faint diamond pattern in the field.

## What did not change

- **Sizes and positions**: each sofa is still exactly 880 deep x 2250 long at its planned centre in every layout, the back
  is 925 mm high and the cushions top out where the old ones did, so clicking a sofa still reports
  2,250 x 880 x 970 mm. The rug is the same size and 15 mm thick. A new test (`tests/sofa-models.test.mjs`) measures every
  layout's sofas and rug against the room plan, so this cannot drift unnoticed.
- **Colours**: cream frame (#e6dac6), lighter cream cushions (#efe5d3), dark wood legs (#4a2f1e), terracotta / ochre /
  sand cushions, terracotta and tan rug.
- The sofa itself (frame, seat and back cushions, legs) still goes to the Blender export; the scatter cushions and the rug
  are still left out of it, as before.
- Coffee table, lamp tables, curtains, plant and wall panels are untouched here (other work covers tables and plants).

## Choices made without asking

- **Proportions inside the 880 x 2250 box** (a model choice, not from a plan or a shop): seat height about 443 mm (was
  about 560), seat depth about 570 mm in front of the back cushions, arms 655 mm high and 170 mm wide, legs 100 mm.
- **Scatter cushion sizes and arrangement**: 550, 480 and 420 mm squares, symmetric. Change them in `DEFAULT_SCATTER` in
  `src/rooms/shared/furniture/Sofa.js`.
- **Rug pattern**: the light guard stripe and the faint diamond lattice are a suggestion; the two colours are yours.
- No throw blanket was added: one draped over an arm would hang outside the sofa's planned footprint.

## Speed

Measured in a headless browser on this machine while two other jobs were running, so the numbers move a lot between runs.
- Drawing Room page: 74.8-74.9 frames per second before and after in every layout. That is the display's limit, so it says
  only that the page stays smooth. Turning the sofas and rug on and off in the same session added about 2 ms per frame both
  before and after.
- Whole home 3D: 22-32 frames per second before and 20-31 after, across the runs. Turning the sofas and rug on and off in
  the same session made no measurable difference before or after.
- Each sofa is now about 6,700 triangles in 6 parts (was 23 plain boxes). Sofas share their shapes and materials.

## Not verified

- The Blender (archviz) export was not run with the new sofas. The fabric texture is generated in the app, not loaded
  from a file, and the exporter should carry it, but this was not tried.
- Draft and Standard quality, Evening mode, the palette preview and the review-sheet capture were not looked at; only the
  automatic quality level on this machine (High).
- This is a look, not a product: no particular sofa model was matched.
