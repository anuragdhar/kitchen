# Main entry: stainless outer door, round lights, and the "4th section" question (2026-10-04)

Three things were asked for the Main entry on 2026-10-04. This note says what changed in the 3D model and the work plan,
which decisions were taken without asking, what the recommendation for the fourth section is, and what has not been checked.
Everything is drawn from the A501 floor plan; nothing in the entry has been measured on site.

## How the model reads the entry

The entry on the plan is an L-shaped space in the north-west corner of the home. Walking in, it has four parts, and the model
now names them in this order (`entrySections()` in `react-configurator/src/domain/entryFittings.mjs`):

| # | Section | Size from the plan | What is in it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Corridor from the outer door to the wooden main (arrival) door | about 2,170 long x 1,290 wide (the plan dimension says 7 ft = 2,134; the owner says about 1.0 m clear) | the first (outer) door on the west, the services shaft cover on the right, the PVC plank ceiling that is planned |
| 2 | Services shaft | about 2,170 x 700 | behind the plain iron cover; not walked into |
| 3 | Pocket behind the Drawing Room wall | about 2,130 x 985 | the east cabinet (open to the gallery) and the west cabinet (reached from the Drawing Room) |
| 4 | Inner gallery from the arrival door to the Drawing Room door | about 1,150 wide x 3,200 long (the plan dimension says 1,000 wide) | shoe rack with mirror doors at the north end, the fold-down seat, the key tray, the open face of the east cabinet, the wooden door into the Drawing Room at the south end |

"The 4th section" in the owner's question is read as the inner gallery (part 4). If the owner meant something else, the answer
below still applies to the gallery, which is the only part of the entry that had no light planned.

## 1. The first (outer) door is now a ventilated stainless steel door

Before: the model drew only the opening in the west wall of the corridor (about 905 mm wide, 2,200 high, from the plan), with
no door leaf at all. The work plan said "replace the iron door with stainless steel" and nothing about ventilation.

After: the opening holds a stainless steel door, drawn closed, in both the Main entry page and Whole home 3D
(`ENTRY.outerDoor` in `react-configurator/src/config/entryConfig.js`, built by `src/rooms/entry/EntryOuterDoor.js`):

- Frame of 45 mm box section on both jambs and over the head; one leaf 815 wide x 2,145 high in 40 mm box section, with a
  10 mm gap at the floor.
- From the bottom up: a solid kick plate 0-300; a grille of 12 mm square bars at 100 mm spacing with a fine stainless insect
  mesh behind it, 300-925; a solid lock rail 925-1,075; grille again 1,075-2,000; a solid top rail 2,000-2,145.
- Free air through the door: about 0.67 square metres, 38% of the leaf (bars let 88% through, the mesh about 60% of that).
  A plain louvred or perforated sheet would give less; the check `checkEntryOuterDoor()` asks for at least 30%.
- Lock at 1,000 mm on the solid rail: a mortise lock with lever handles on both faces, a deadbolt and a night latch, keyed
  from both sides. Grade 304 (316 near the coast), brushed finish.
- Hung on the north jamb and opening OUT onto the landing, the same hand as the wooden arrival door. This is a working
  choice (`hingeKnown: false`); the fabricator and the owner settle it on site.

Not changed, as decided: the wooden arrival door at the end of the corridor and the wooden door into the Drawing Room are
exactly as before (the Drawing Room door keeps the air-conditioned area small), and no second wooden door is added behind the
steel one. The work-plan task "Replace the iron main entry door" now carries the ventilated design and what is still to choose;
open item C9 records what is decided and what is open.

## 2. Round lights instead of linear ones

Where the linear lights came from: the live 3D views carry a generic "Interior studio" lighting proposal that draws, in every
room, a long glowing strip along one wall (a cove), a thin strip along another (a cabinet light) and a vertical bar (an accent).
In the entry these showed as linear lights on the Main entry page and in Whole home 3D. The earlier owner instruction for
round lights is in the work plan (task "Finalise the entry ceiling", 2026-10-04, and open item C6): recessed round LED panel
lights, the Orient Electric type already used in the home, single colour, proposed neutral white 4000 K, two of 6-8 W along
the corridor on one switch at the door.

After: the generic strips are switched off for the entry (the same rule the Drawing Room already uses), and the entry draws its
own round panel lights from `react-configurator/src/config/entryLightingConfig.js`, built by `src/rooms/entry/EntryCeilingLights.js`:

| Light | Where | Type | Position (from the plan) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Corridor (section 1) | recessed round panel, 8 W, 800 lm, 120 mm with a 105 mm cut-out | centre line of the corridor, about 540 mm in from the outer door wall |
| E2 | Corridor (section 1) | same | centre line, about 540 mm in from the arrival door wall; 1.1 m from E1 |
| E3 | Inner gallery (section 4) | surface-mounted round panel, 8 W, 800 lm, 170 mm across, 35 mm deep | centre of the gallery, about 780 mm in front of the shoe rack's mirror doors, over the fold-down seat and the key tray |
| E4 | Inner gallery (section 4) | same | centre of the gallery, about 660 mm from the Drawing Room wall, in front of the open east cabinet |

Totals: four round lights, 32 W, about 3,200 lumens; about 570 lm per square metre in the corridor and 430 in the gallery.
Nothing linear remains in the entry. The lights are drawn at the slab (2,700) because how far the PVC ceiling drops is not
decided (open item C5). Switching as proposed: E1 and E2 on the owner's switch beside the door; E3 and E4 on their own switch
beside the Drawing Room door (a two-way switch at the arrival door is an option).

Decisions taken without asking: 8 W per light (inside the owner's 6-8 W); neutral white 4000 K as proposed, not yet agreed;
the two gallery lights and their switch (see the next section); surface-mounted type in the gallery because it keeps its plaster
ceiling; and the exact positions. All are easy to move in the config.

## 3. Does the 4th section need a spotlight or a track light? Recommendation: no

The inner gallery does need its own light: it had none, and the two corridor lights are behind a door and round a corner from
it. But the right fitting there is the same round panel as in the corridor, not a spotlight and not a track:

- The gallery is only about 1.0-1.15 m wide. A surface track with its heads hangs 120-150 mm below the slab, right at eye
  level for anyone tall in such a narrow passage, and spot heads would reflect in the mirror doors of the shoe rack.
- What needs light there is fixed and known: the mirror (a person's face in front of it), the seat where shoes are changed, the
  key tray, the cabinet doors. Two round panels placed over those spots do this evenly. A track's strength, moving heads later,
  is not needed; the Drawing Room and Lobby have tracks because their furniture and art may change.
- A spotlight aimed at the mirror doors would light the glass, not the person; the panel in front of the mirror (E3) lights the
  face. If the owner wants an accent later, a small LED strip inside the east cabinet is the useful one, not a ceiling spot.
- Cost and wiring: two more points on the same lighting circuit, versus a track, driver and dimmer for one small passage.

So no track was modelled, and no dimmer. If a track is wanted anyway, it should follow the Lobby pattern (one 48 V magnetic
track, one dimmer for the whole track; its heads cannot be dimmed separately).
