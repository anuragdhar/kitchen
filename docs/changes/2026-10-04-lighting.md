# Lighting, 2026-10-04: one dimmer per track, Lobby strips removed, tracks in four more rooms

## What changed, in plain words

1. **One track = one circuit.** You told us the heads on a track cannot be dimmed one by one, only the whole track. The
   model now works that way everywhere: every track run has its own 48 V driver and its own wall dimmer, and the sliders
   on the room pages are one per circuit (Drawing Room: Chandelier / Track 1 / Track 2; Lobby: Dining pendant / Lobby
   track 1 / Lobby track 2). Track 2 in the Drawing Room keeps its reading and diffused heads together: turn the whole
   track up to read, down for TV; the chandelier carries the general light. No head or track moved. The single 150 W
   driver of the Drawing Room becomes a 60 W (Track 1) and a 100 W (Track 2); the Lobby's 100 W becomes two 60 W.
2. **Lobby strip lights removed.** The white strips along the north and south walls and in the Pooja alcove were leftovers
   of the generic "proposed lighting" overlay from the earlier design (`src/render/interiorLighting.js`). They are no
   longer drawn in the Lobby or the Pooja alcove, on the room page or in Whole home 3D. The dining pendant, the two
   tracks, and the warm light inside the Pooja unit stay. The overlay's invisible area lights stay too (they only add
   light, no fixtures).
3. **Track lights in Bedroom 1, Bedroom 3, the Study (Bedroom 2) and the Kitchen**, designed the same way, with the
   same checks and per-circuit sliders. Details below. The kids layout of the Study and the balcony office were not
   touched; the balcony office gets no track from here.

## Decisions taken without asking (change any of them in the config files)

- Driver sizes: the next standard step (60 W, 100 W) above each run's load divided by 0.8. Bedroom 3's north track sits
  at 77% of a 60 W driver; a 100 W one gives more room for an extra head later.
- Ceiling fans in Bedroom 1, Bedroom 3 and the Study are **assumed** at the room centre with 1200 mm blades hanging
  300 mm, and drawn so you can see the assumption (`status: 'assumed'`). Bedroom 3's fan is one field,
  `BEDROOM3_CEILING_FAN` in `bedroom3LightingConfig.js`, to be patched from the scan; the checks pass with it anywhere
  within 400 mm of the centre. The kitchen has no ceiling fan in the model.
- Bed reading in Bedroom 3 and the Study: because the fan is over the bed, the reading heads sit on the north/south (or
  foot) side and are **aimed** at the sleeper's chest with a gentle tilt rather than hanging straight over the pillows.
  This is a new, generic option on reading heads (`targetMm`, `targetHeightMm`) checked for tilt (35 degrees at most).
- Bedroom 1 puts its reading heads straight down over the bed, 750 mm from the headboard, on a short track of their own
  with a spot on the headboard wall; the wardrobe track carries the general light.
- Kitchen colour 4000 K (neutral white, a work room); the other rooms stay 3000 K. The kitchen track has no wall spots
  (every wall has cabinets); the check accepts that for this room only.
- Kitchen under-cabinet LED strips are kept and made a dimmable circuit in the 3D view; a ceiling light cannot reach the
  back of a counter under a wall cabinet that goes to the ceiling.
- Whole home 3D shows the new tracks and the assumed fans in all four rooms (fixtures only, no extra lights, as for the
  Lobby).

## Removed

- The generic overlay's cove, cabinet and accent strip meshes in: Lobby, Pooja alcove, Bedroom 1, Bedroom 3, Study,
  Kitchen (and the Drawing Room, already). Nothing else was removed. The vanity mirror glow in Bedroom 3, the Pooja
  interior light, the Study's fill light and the kitchen's own LED strips and task glows all stay.

- The kitchen's 3D scene is rebuilt whenever the layout changes; the sliders then show their old values while the
  rebuilt lights start at the planned level (move a slider to resync).
- Fan positions in Bedroom 1, Bedroom 3 and the Study are assumed (see above). Lobby fan still unconfirmed (C22).
- Measurements are from the model, not from site; the Study targets mirror the drawn kids layout, which has no config
