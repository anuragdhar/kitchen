# One palette for the whole home (2026-10-05)

Plain-language note for the owner. Nothing in any room was changed: no size, no position, no saved layout, and the 3D
views look exactly as before unless you choose a palette to preview.

## What was added

- A **Palette** page, opened from its card on the home page: the recommended palette and two alternatives next to today's
  finishes, each with swatches, what to ask a dealer for, a room-by-room table, the list of what would change from
  today, and the result of the checks.
- A **palette preview** for the live 3D views (on the Palette page, and under Interior studio > Materials). "Today" is
  the default. A chosen palette re-colours cabinetry, doors and room walls in every room and in Whole home 3D. Floors,
  fabrics, handles, tiles, lamp colour and the fluted panel wall do not change in 3D yet (see "Not done").
- `docs/PALETTE.md`: the full write-up, including the list of every finish the app uses today and where it came from.
- Config and checks: `react-configurator/src/config/homePaletteConfig.js`, `src/home/palette.mjs`, and 13 tests in
  `tests/home-palette.test.mjs`.

## The recommendation in five lines

1. **Honey oak** (the shade you chose for Bedroom 3) for all cabinetry, doors, the TV console, the fluted panel wall and
   the south window frame: one laminate for the home. The kitchen keeps its light oak.
2. **Warm ivory walls**, one **soft sand** wall in each main room, flat white ceilings.
3. **Cream fabric** with **terracotta** and **olive** accents: the Drawing Room colours carried through the home, and
   the olive ties in the Bedroom 3 wardrobe that is staying.
4. **Brushed stainless** handles everywhere, to go with the entry door; brass only in the Pooja unit; dark walnut only
   for loose furniture and the carved panels.
5. **3000 K** light everywhere, **4000 K** in the kitchen, 2700 K in the Pooja unit. The entry moves from the proposed
   4000 K to 3000 K, because it opens straight into the Drawing Room.

It changes none of your decisions. The only rows it flags are the existing wooden doors, which would be re-polished to
the honey tone. The two alternatives (Light oak and sage; Teak and brass) do change decisions and say where.

## Defaults chosen without asking

- Honey oak is drawn as `#b27d4c`, the colour of the Bedroom 3 fronts in the model, not a measured sample.
- The olive of the existing wardrobe (`#6f7350`) and the floor (`#d9cfc0`) are **estimates**: neither is recorded
  anywhere in the project. No palette asks for new flooring.
- Kitchen tile and worktop are kept as drawn in every palette; no dated decision about either was found.
- Limits used by the checks: 2 wood tones per room, 3 in the home, 2 metal finishes, wall-to-wood contrast at least
  1.3 : 1, one main fabric plus up to three accents; "warm" means 2700-3000 K in living rooms and bedrooms.
- Buying specifications name the kind of product (for example 1 mm matt laminate on BWP plywood, premium washable
  emulsion) and a shade family. No catalogue codes: pick them from the dealer's shade card with samples in hand.
- The preview choice is kept in this browser only, under its own key; it never rewrites the saved wood and plaster
  setting, and going back to Today restores it.

## Not done, and why

- Floors, fabrics, handles, tiles, the fluted panel wall, the TV console, accent walls and lamp colour are not
  re-coloured in 3D. They are plain colours inside the room builders, which other work is changing at the moment; what
  it would take is listed at the end of `docs/PALETTE.md`.
- The existing wardrobe in Bedroom 3 is still drawn beige and shown as wood, not olive.
- `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` and the work plan were not edited (see below).

## Not verified

- No colour was checked against a real sample, the real floor, or the real wardrobe. Screen colours are a guide only.
- The 3D wood preview tints the nearest bundled veneer picture; it shows tone, not a supplier's grain.
- Screenshots looked at: the Palette page (desktop and phone width, all four palettes on desktop), Drawing Room,
  Bedroom 3 and Whole home 3D with the default look (the differences from the pictures taken before the change are the
  same size as between two runs of the same code), and Drawing Room, Bedroom 3, Lobby and Whole home 3D with a palette
  selected. The kitchen, Study, entry, storage and balcony office pages were not opened with a palette selected.
- The browser suites (`test:browser`, `test:persistence`, `test:materials`, `test:lighting`, `visual:qa`) were not run.
