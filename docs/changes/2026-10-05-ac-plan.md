# One air-conditioning plan for the whole home (2026-10-05)

Until now the app had AC bits in several places: outdoor units at your four plan marks, your window AC in the Bedroom 1
balcony, the shoe rack idea, and a few indoor units drawn as concepts. This change joins them into one plan, checks it,
and answers the four open questions. The full plan is `docs/AC_PLAN.md`. It is a proposal from typical figures; nothing
is measured, and a dealer and an installer must confirm it.

## The four answers

1. **Drawing Room outdoor unit: the west wall, not the shoe rack.** 4.3 m of pipe against about 11 m, and the shoe rack
   keeps all its storage. The indoor unit stays on the west wall where the electrical plan already has its power point.
   Two things to settle with the installer first: how the outdoor unit is reached for service (there is no window on
   that wall; from the roof by rope is the likely way), and where the drain goes (10 m along the outside as drawn).
2. **Bedroom 1 does not need a split AC.** Your 1.5 ton window AC covers the bedroom and balcony (about 1.05 ton needed).
   The outdoor unit you marked outside the kitchen wall is still in the app, marked "not needed for Bedroom 1". It is
   noisier at night than a split; if that bothers you, the fallback is in `docs/AC_PLAN.md`.
3. **The Lobby needs its own AC** if you want the dining area cool with the glass doors closed: the Drawing Room unit
   cannot do both rooms. Its outdoor unit goes in the place you marked for Bedroom 1, which is now free. Even if you buy
   the machine later, the pipes, drain and power point must go in now, before plaster and woodwork.
4. **Bedroom 2's indoor unit belongs on the Study west wall just north of the Home Office opening.** It is the only wall
   with room. From there it blows across onto the bed as drawn, and the Study plus Home Office is a little too much for
   1.5 ton on hot afternoons: sun film or blinds on the Home Office west glass helps most.

The kitchen gets no AC: hob heat, the chimney and grease make it a poor place for one.
