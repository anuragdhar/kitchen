# Site scan, Drawing Room and Lobby / Dining (2026-10-04)

The owner scanned both rooms with a phone (Scaniverse, LiDAR) and exported `Scaniverse 2026-10-04 194853.glb`
(17.7 MB, 198,132 vertices, one 8192 px texture; kept in the owner's Downloads folder, not in this repository).
This file records what was measured from it, what was put into the app, and what was deliberately left alone.

## How it was measured

- The mesh was rotated 8.95 degrees about the vertical axis so the walls line up with the axes, then sampled to a
  coloured point cloud at about 5 mm spacing. Orientation was fixed from the plan: the Lobby is east of the Drawing Room
  and the Drawing Room window is on the south wall.
- Wall, jamb, sill and soffit positions are the peaks of point histograms along each surface's normal (5 mm bins).
- Small items (switch plates, ceiling rosettes, the window frame) were read off textured orthographic views at
  250-300 pixels per metre.
- Phone LiDAR is usually good to 1-2 %. Treat room sizes as +/- 30-50 mm and small items as +/- 20 mm. Nothing here
  replaces a tape for anything that will be cut or ordered.
- Not captured: the fans and the chandelier (the scan flattened them into the ceiling), anything hidden by curtains or
  furniture, wall thickness, and what is behind a surface.

Units are millimetres. Positions are from the room's west and north walls; heights are above the floor.
For the Lobby, "from the west" is from the Drawing Room face of the beam between the two rooms, about 40 mm west of
the Lobby's x = 0 in `roomShellConfig.js`.

## Left alone, and why

- **Room width, length and height, and the beam.** The whole-home model draws every room inside the A501 plan outline
  with one 2700 mm ceiling. Changing two rooms alone would put their ceiling fittings above the whole-home ceiling and
  shift furniture checked against the 3353 mm width. The 203 mm width difference may also be a centre-line versus
  wall-face difference on the plan, so it needs a tape before the model is rebuilt around it.
- Because the width is unchanged, items measured from the west wall keep their west-wall distance. The window therefore
  ends 420 mm from the model's east wall where the real one ends 217 mm from it.
- **Lobby north door**: the app shows the planned position.
- **Lobby east opening**: within 75 mm of the app; not worth moving on scan accuracy alone.
- **Existing switchboards and sockets**: recorded above, not added to the proposed electrical plan.
