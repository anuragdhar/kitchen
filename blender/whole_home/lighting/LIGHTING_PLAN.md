# A501 lighting and finish concept

This is a visual design study based on the current app model. It keeps the Drawing Room's **existing chandelier as its only ceiling fixture**. The beige plaster is Poly Haven's CC0 Beige Wall 001 material, with a sand-toned ceiling and pale limestone-like floor. The cabinet, furniture, and kitchen geometry remain schematic.

| Area | General light | Task and accent light |
|---|---|---|
| Drawing Room | Existing chandelier above the oval table | West wall wash, window-seat reading light, subtle TV bias light |
| Lobby / Dining | Warm pendant or surface light near dining table | Dining task light; Pooja opening can have its own accent circuit |
| Bedroom 1 | Soft surface-mounted ceiling light | Bedside reading and balcony desk light |
| Bedroom 3 | Soft surface-mounted ceiling light | Integrated vanity mirror and bedside task light |
| Study / Bedroom 2 | Low-profile ceiling light | Desk light; independent light for the balcony office |
| Kitchen | Slim surface-mounted ambient light | Counter task lighting on both sides, controlled separately |
| Main entry | Compact surface light | Shoe storage accent |
| Balcony office | Compact ambient light | Desk task light |
| Terrace | Exterior-rated general light | Warm wall accent, controlled separately |
| Pooja Ghar | Warm ambient light above the alcove | Focused altar light, controlled separately |
| Storage by fridge | Compact surface or sensor light | Light the rolling-rack fronts without a moving wire |
| Bedroom 1 enclosed balcony | Soft ambient light | Desk task light facing the glazing |

The room previews show these layers as concepts. They do not represent final electrical locations or brightness calculations. Select fixtures and power supplies after actual site measurements, daylight review, and switch planning. The overhead tour is a guided view above the walls, not an eye-level route through doors.

Open `A501-whole-home-lighting.blend` in Blender to edit materials, lights, fixtures and camera animation. Room stills and the tour use the same saved home model.
